## Context
Decyde started as a practical response to the same repeated problem in friend groups: too much time deciding what to watch and not enough time actually watching.

## Approach
I treated the first release as a product foundation project. The experience focused on quick room setup, fast voting loops, and clear visibility into what has already been watched.

## Result
The current build provides a stable base for expansion into richer recommendation and history features.
