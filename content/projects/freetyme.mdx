## Context
Freetyme was designed to remove repetitive back-and-forth when organizing group activities.

## Approach
The product model focused on declaring constraints once and surfacing overlap windows with minimal manual interpretation.

## Result
The application currently serves as an active prototype with strong UX and data-model direction for future iterations.
