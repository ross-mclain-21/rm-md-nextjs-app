## Context
Freetyme is an alpha-stage app built for groups that want to get together without the hassle of endless scheduling messages. The goal is simple: help friends quickly find free time where their calendars overlap and turn that opening into a real plan.

## Approach
Users can import their calendars, choose how long of a time slot they need, and surface the best overlapping options. From there, a proposed slot can be shared directly in the chat so everyone can vote on what works best.

## Result
When a winning time is selected, the event can be added to everyone's calendar. Freetyme also includes a movie night app that helps groups pick a winner for what to watch, and it sets the direction for more apps that are already planned as the platform continues to expand.
